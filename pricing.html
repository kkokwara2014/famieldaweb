Good to know

Billing stays in the workspace, not on this website.

Create an account, then open Settings → Subscription to start Plus monthly or yearly. Stripe handles the card. Famielda never stores the full card number. Cancel anytime; Plus stays on through the end of the period.

Who pays?

The household that runs the senior workspace. Caregivers and practitioners do not pay Famielda to accept an invitation.

Invoices

Monthly or annual Plus invoices appear in Settings after checkout. You can also open the Stripe Customer Portal from there.

If payment fails

Plus shows as past due. Update the card in Settings. Access continues if payment is repaired before the period ends.

Not a medical bill

Famielda is software for coordination. It is not insurance, not a clinic fee, and not a charge for a home-care shift.
