What families use Famielda for

Coverage you can see, not coverage you hope for.

The household in one circle

Invite siblings, a spouse, or an adult child. Free includes two family members. Plus brings the rest of the household in.

Professionals when you need them

Add a caregiver or practitioner on Plus. Verification helps you see that Famielda has reviewed their credentials.

A week that does not live in chat

Rides, visits, and reminders sit on one schedule. When someone checks in, the family can see it.

Medications and papers

Plus keeps a shared medication list, care history, and a private document vault — never a public link.

Alerts that matter

Invitations, visit changes, and emergency status land in one inbox. You choose the quieter reminders.

Invite relatives who are not here yet

Family referral invites help another relative join Famielda as family, with a Plus trial when that path is live.

Your role

Family members run the household workspace.

After you sign up, choose Family Member. Create the senior profile, invite the circle, and keep the week honest. Caregivers and practitioners join the same record — they do not replace you as the people who love them.

  • Same email opens Famielda Web and Famielda Mobile
  • Role-based access so not every note is for every person
  • Upgrade in Settings when the circle needs Plus

Know this

Famielda does not diagnose or prescribe.

Medication on Plus is a shared list the circle coordinates — name, dose, timing, and history. Clinical decisions stay with licensed practitioners and the family.
