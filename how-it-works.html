How it works

From first profile to a week the circle can trust.

Famielda is not another group chat. It is a shared workspace with a clear order: the person, the people around them, then the week.

Create your workspace I’m coordinating for family

Step 1

Establish the senior profile.

Start with the person you are caring for. Free includes one senior. The profile is the source of truth for contacts, routines, and — on Plus — medications, documents, and care history.

  • One place for the details that used to live in a binder
  • Visible to the circle according to each person’s role
  • Ready for caregivers and practitioners to join later

Step 2

Invite the people already helping.

Family members join by email. Caregivers and health practitioners complete role setup and can submit verification so the household can see a Famielda-reviewed badge.

  • Free includes two family members
  • Plus opens caregivers, practitioners, and a larger household
  • Each person lands in the workspace that matches their role

Step 3

Plan the week, then see what happened.

Schedule visits against real availability. Famielda rejects overlapping bookings for the same professional. Check-in, visit notes, and alerts keep the family in the loop when they cannot be in the room.

  • Week view built for caregiving, not a generic calendar
  • Overlap protection across households
  • Notifications for invitations, visits, and urgent status
