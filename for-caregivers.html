Your own workspace

Sign up, choose Caregiver, and pick CNA, CMT, or another caregiving credential. You land on a caregiver dashboard — not a generic inbox.

Availability that blocks double-booking

Set when you can work. Famielda rejects overlapping visits, including two families trying to book you at the same time.

Check-in and visit notes

Arrive, check in, complete the visit, and leave a note the circle can read. The family does not have to text you for proof of presence.

Professional verification

Upload identity and credential documents. When Famielda verifies you, households see a badge before they invite you into the home.

More than one household

Free allows one active family relationship. Plus lets you work with more households — still never two visits at once.

Messages with the circle

Coordination stays in Famielda, with the same account on web and mobile for days you are not at a desk.

How families find you

You join a circle by invitation.

A family invites you by email from Care Circle. You create or sign in to a Famielda account, accept, and the visit workflow opens. You do not scrape a public directory — access is household by household.

  • Inviting caregivers is a Famielda Plus feature for the household
  • Your verification status is visible to the circle
  • Schedule changes and cancellations stay on the visit record
