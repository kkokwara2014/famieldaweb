About

Care should not depend on who remembered to text.

Famielda exists so families can know their person is cared for — even when work, distance, or the rest of life keeps them from being in the room. We build the workspace around that promise.

Why we built this

Know they're cared for, even when you can't be there.

What Famielda is

A governed home for family caregiving.

One senior profile, one care circle, one schedule, and one record. Family members, caregivers, and health practitioners each get a workspace that matches how they show up — with access the browser cannot override.

Family first

The household owns the circle. Professionals are invited in. Coverage is planned, not pieced together in a group chat.

Roles, not a shared password

Family, caregiver, practitioner, and admin are first-class. Clinical notes and documents follow those roles.

Trust before the doorbell

Caregivers and practitioners can submit identity and license documents. Famielda reviews them before the verified badge appears.

Privacy as a default

Care data belongs to the signed-in circle. Documents live in a private vault. Card numbers stay with Stripe. Read the Privacy Policy.

What Famielda is not

We coordinate care. We do not replace it.

  • Not a hospital, clinic, or emergency service
  • Not a pharmacy, and not a system that diagnoses or prescribes
  • Not a public directory of caregivers
  • Not a substitute for licensed clinical judgment

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Famielda helps the circle stay aligned around ongoing care.

Web and mobile

One account for the whole family stack.

Famielda Web and Famielda Mobile share the same login. The public site you are on now is separate from the signed-in workspace — it is how people find Famielda, understand it, and join.
